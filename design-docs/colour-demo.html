Colour by recursive subdivision

Every node owns a region of colour space; its children divide that region. No colour is hand-picked: every cell names the SAME role (surface) and reads it from a palette transposed by the product of the offsets along that node's path. Real rich-js themes, real transposePalette. The top row is today's hue.step for comparison. Drag hidden leaves — it recolours row 2 under hue.step and cannot touch it under subdivision.
