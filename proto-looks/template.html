Accent variation: tuning candidates

Three things to pick, each a control below: jump, how far apart neighbouring segments sit in lightness; keystone, which places along each line wear the accent; open, the colour a menu wears when open — its header's colour, which its items are shades of.

Every closed cell is a colour the theme names: its line's role at a shade of the window, or the accent at a keystone. Every menu item is the header's hue at a shade of the same window, so a menu is a variation on the colour that opened it. The first strip under each theme is the bar as it ships today. Compare along stacks every value of one choice under each theme. A keystone sits on a line's authored end, so when the last cell hides (the activity cell, shown only while Claude works), that end is bare — see the payloads.
